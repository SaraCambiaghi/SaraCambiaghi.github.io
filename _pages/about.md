I am a final-year PhD student at the University of Pavia, working within the 
[CompOpt](https://www.compopt.it/) group. My field is operations research, 
in particular applied to healthcare and scheduling. 

My PhD research project is _Optimization and machine learning for the efficiency of public hospital services_. The project focuses on the study of combinatorial optimization methods and data science models to support decision-making in the management of healthcare services, with the goal of improving the efficiency of resource utilization from the perspective of multiple stakeholders: patients (service quality and fairness), healthcare professionals (workload distribution), and hospital management (resource utilization and throughput).
